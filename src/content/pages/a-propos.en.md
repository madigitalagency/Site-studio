## One person, Paris and Brussels

The Studio is the video workshop of M&A Digital Agency, a one-person agency. No team to introduce, no face on screen: the films speak, and the behind-the-scenes say how they are made.

## The background, without the photo

A legal education, which left a taste for verified sources and proofread copy. Years of customer service in aviation and tourism, where you learn what a traveller remembers of a brand and what they forgive. A knowledge of emerging markets, French-speaking ones first. And, since 2024, making content and websites for demanding companies, with AI in support.

ROUVRAY comes from there: early commercial aviation, told with true facts. So does VARENNE, in its own way: what an article does to a brand, and to the woman who carries it.

## The method

A short film is made shot by shot. We write, record the voice, approve one still image per shot, animate, sort, edit. The generated image decides nothing: the start frame, the shot list and the edit make the film. All of it is detailed in the [Method](/en/method/), and every trade notion has its page in the [Glossary](/en/glossary/).

## The tools, by role

Image generation, video generation from still images, synthetic voices, music generated under a commercial licence, editing, grading and subtitling. The tools change every quarter; the method does not. That is why we name them by what they do, not by their brand.

## What we do not do

No real person imitated, no real brand without its consent, no real place presented as real. No film without its "AI-generated images" disclosure. No client named without written consent.
