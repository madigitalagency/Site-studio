## In two sentences

This site sets no cookies and uses no trackers. The only personal data processed is what you send us through the contact form, and the server's technical logs.

## The contact form

**Data collected**: name, email address, company (optional), project type, indicative budget, message, and the page language.

**Purpose**: to answer your request for a quote or information.

**Legal basis**: pre-contractual steps taken at your request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) and your consent, ticked before sending.

**Recipients**: the person in charge of M&A Digital Agency. The email is routed by our automation tool, hosted on our own server, then by a transactional email provider established in the European Union, acting as a processor.

**Retention**: three years after the last exchange, then deletion.

**Your rights**: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, by writing to contact@madigitalagency.net. You may also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr).

## Server logs

For every page requested, the server records the IP address, the date, the page and the browser. These logs are used for security and for cookie-free audience measurement, done on our own machines. They are kept for six months at most, then automatically deleted.

## What is not done

No font, video or script is loaded from a third party. No social network is embedded in the pages: links to Instagram or TikTok are plain links. No data is sold or shared for advertising purposes.
